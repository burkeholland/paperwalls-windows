# Paper privacy policy

Paper does not collect personal data, track usage, display ads, or send analytics or crash reports.

## Network access

The app downloads its wallpaper catalog and images from the Paper collection on GitHub Pages (`https://burkeholland.github.io/paper/`). These requests are sent directly to GitHub. Like any website host, GitHub receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address. GitHub handles these requests under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Local storage

Favorites, rotation settings, the cached catalog, and downloaded wallpapers stay on your device in the app's local storage. The image cache is limited to 750 MB. You can clear downloaded wallpapers in Settings; the current wallpaper is kept. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data.

Launch at login is optional, off by default, and controlled through Windows Startup settings. When the window is closed, Paper stays in the system tray so wallpaper rotation can continue. Choose **Quit** in the tray menu to stop it.

## Questions

Report privacy questions through the [Paper repository](https://github.com/burkeholland/paperwalls-windows/issues). Do not include private information in public issues.
